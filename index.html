The Event

What to Expect

STOIX Padel Cup is a private, invitation-style tournament designed for senior leaders in the PE and portfolio company space. No big stages. No name badges. Just great padel and genuine connection.

🏆
Tournament Format
A structured round-robin tournament across four hours, organised and facilitated by Padel Box. All ability levels welcome — this is about the people, not the scorecard.
🤝
Curated Company
Limited to 16 teams — 8 spots remaining. Partners, Portfolio Directors, Operating Partners and CFOs from PE firms and their portfolio companies. Quality over quantity, always.
🥂
Post-Match Reception
The tournament wraps into an informal drinks reception. The best conversations of the day usually happen here. No agenda — just good people winding down.
❤️

Playing for Something Bigger

STOIX Padel Cup isn't just a day of sport — it's a chance to do something meaningful while you're at it. All proceeds from the event go directly to charity. We'll share more details on the charity partner shortly. If you'd like to get involved in the fundraising side, let us know when you register.

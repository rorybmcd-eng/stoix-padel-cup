**📅 The basics**

- **Date:** Thursday 25 June 2026
- **Time:** Arrive from 1:45 pm. Play starts at 2:00 pm sharp.
- **Finish:** Tournament wraps around 5:30 pm, followed by drinks and a brief prize ceremony.
- **Venue:** Padel Box, Bermondsey, London SE15

The day will be run by the team at Padel Box in a round-robin format across multiple courts. All ability levels are playing, so don't worry if you're not a regular — the point is to have a great afternoon.

**What to bring:**
- Suitable court shoes (padel, squash, or tennis shoes — no trainers with black soles)
- Comfortable sports clothing
- A water bottle
- Any medication or personal items you need for exercise

Rackets are provided. Padel balls are provided.
